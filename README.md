# FastFoodFinder
**FastFoodFinder** is an app that is designed to help people find healthier options at fast food restaurants without having to search through every menu item on their own. A lot of fast food restaurants have nutrition 
information available, but it can take time to look through everything and compare different choices. FastFoodFinder would make that process easier by putting the information in one place and allowing users to sort 
and filter menu items based on what they are looking for.

### **Problem the Project Solves**
The problem **FastFoodFinder** is trying to solve is that it can be difficult to make healthier choices when eating fast food. People may want something with fewer calories, more protein, or less fat, but finding that 
information can take too long. Each restaurant also displays nutrition information differently. My app would make it easier by giving users a simple way to compare menu items and quickly find options that fit their needs.

### **Target Users**
The main target users for **FastFoodFinder** would be people who eat fast food but still want to pay attention to their nutrition. This could include people who go to the gym, people trying to lose weight, people tracking 
calories or protein, or anyone who just wants to make a healthier choice when eating out. It could also be useful for busy people who do not want to spend a lot of time looking through different restaurant websites.
